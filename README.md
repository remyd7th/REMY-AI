# Remy AI

**Your intelligent AI work assistant.**

Remy AI helps Executive Assistants, Virtual Assistants, professionals and businesses organize their work, manage communication, coordinate schedules, handle administrative activities and stay on top of their responsibilities — while keeping humans in control.

> Remy helps the user organize, assist, suggest, and execute — while the user remains in control.

Full spec: [`Docs/Remy AI.md`](Docs/Remy%20AI.md)

## Who it's for

**Primary:**
- Executive Assistants
- Virtual Assistants

**Secondary:** small business owners, entrepreneurs, founders, managers, professionals, teams, larger organizations.

## Core experience

- **Chat** — natural-language work instructions (e.g. "organize my tasks for tomorrow")
- **Voice** — hands-free reminders and actions
- **Dashboard** — tasks, meetings, emails, follow-ups, documents, payments, progress

## MVP focus

1. AI Chat Assistant
2. Task Management (create, organize, prioritize, track)
3. Calendar & Scheduling (meetings, reminders, conflicts, rescheduling)
4. Email Assistance (organize, summarize, draft, follow-ups)
5. Document Assistance (create, summarize, organize, extract)
6. Follow-Up Management (track outstanding actions, reminders)
7. Dashboard (tasks, meetings, emails, follow-ups, progress)
8. Permission System (user-defined rules, approvals)
9. Personalized Preferences (working style, communication, scheduling)

See the PRD for the full feature breakdown, user journeys, and success criteria.
